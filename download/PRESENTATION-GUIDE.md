# SpagKing — Presentation Guide

**Monday, 10 August 2026**

This guide tells you exactly what to say, what to click, and what the
audience should notice on each slide of the SpagKing Client Presentation.

---

## Slide 1 — Cover

**Say:** "Good morning. Thank you for your time today. What I'm going to
show you is SpagKing's own restaurant technology platform — a complete
digital ecosystem built specifically for how SpagKing operates."

**Do:** Let the cover slide display for 10 seconds. Don't click anything.

**Audience notices:** Gold-on-black premium branding, the SpagKing name,
and the tagline "Customer Experience • Operations • Intelligence."

---

## Slide 2 — The Vision

**Say:** "The vision is simple: one platform that connects every part of
SpagKing — from the customer's first craving to the CEO's morning
dashboard. No more scattered tools. No more blind spots."

**Transition:** "But first, let's talk about the problems this solves."

---

## Slide 3 — The Problem

**Say:** "Today, most restaurants operate with fragmented systems.
Orders come through WhatsApp, phone calls, and walk-ins — with no
unified record. Inventory is tracked on paper. Staff attendance is
manual. And management makes decisions without real-time data."

**Do:** Point to each card briefly. Don't dwell — the audience will
recognize these problems instantly.

**Transition:** "Here's how SpagKing's platform solves all of this."

---

## Slide 4 — The Solution

**Say:** "One connected ecosystem. When a customer orders, the kitchen
sees it instantly. When stock runs low, the manager gets an alert.
When a delivery completes, the CEO's dashboard updates in real-time."

---

## Slide 5 — Customer App

**Transition:** "Let me show you the customer journey."

## Slide 16 — Technology

**Say:** "The platform is built on modern, scalable technology. React,
Next.js, TypeScript — the same stack used by companies like Netflix
and Instagram. It's fast, reliable, and ready to scale."

**Keep it simple.** The audience doesn't need to know what TypeScript
is — they need to know it means "reliable and professional."

---

## Slide 17 — Demo vs Production

**Say:** "Everything you'll see in the demo is fully functional. The
workflows all exist. What needs production setup are the external
connections — live payment credentials, the backend database, WhatsApp
Business API, SMS gateway, and receipt printers."

**Important:** Be transparent. Don't pretend the demo is production-ready.
The client will trust you more for being honest.

## LIVE DEMO INSTRUCTIONS

After the presentation slides, switch to the browser and walk through:

1. **Landing page** — Show the hero, scroll through sections
2. **Click "Order Now"** — Transitions to login
3. **Click "Customer"** — Enters customer app
4. **Browse menu** — Show categories, search, filters
5. **Tap a meal** — Show meal details (chef story, reviews, pairings)
6. **Click "Buy Now"** — Opens cart
7. **Click "Checkout"** — Show delivery/pickup/dine-in, payment methods
8. **Click "Place Order"** — Show the success animation + chef/rider assignment
9. **Order tracking** — Show the animated 7-step timeline
10. **Logout** → Login as **Cashier** → Show POS terminal
11. **Logout** → Login as **CEO** → Show executive dashboard
12. **Logout** → Login as **Inventory** → Show inventory dashboard
13. **Logout** → Login as **HR** → Show staff management

**Pacing:** Spend 2-3 minutes per role. Don't try to show everything —
show the highlights and let the audience ask to see more.

## QUESTIONS THE CLIENT MAY ASK

**Q: "Can we add our own menu items and prices?"**
A: "Yes — the menu is fully customizable. You can add, edit, and price
meals per branch through the admin panel."

**Q: "How long does it take to go live?"**
A: "With the workflows already built, going live is about connecting the
production integrations — payment credentials, backend, WhatsApp —
which takes 4-6 weeks for a pilot branch."

**Q: "Does it work on mobile?"**
A: "Yes — the entire platform is responsive. The customer app works
beautifully on mobile, and staff dashboards work on tablets."

**Q: "Can we use our own payment provider?"**
A: "Yes — the platform supports Paystack and Flutterwave, and can be
extended to any payment provider with an API."

**Q: "What about offline?"**
A: "The current demo requires internet. A PWA/offline mode is on the
roadmap for Phase 2."

**Q: "How much does it cost?"**
A: "That depends on the number of branches and the integrations needed.
We'll provide a detailed quote after the discovery phase."
